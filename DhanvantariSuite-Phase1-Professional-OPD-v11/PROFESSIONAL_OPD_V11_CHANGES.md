# Professional OPD v11

- Reception/Admin New Patient creates a visit in Scheduled/Booked state; it does NOT start consultation.
- Reception must click Check-in when the patient enters the OPD room.
- Check-in changes the centralized visit status and makes the patient available on the assigned doctor dashboard.
- Doctor New Patient may still open consultation directly for a single-doctor workflow.
- Multi-doctor workflow continues to require Consulting Doctor assignment and queue/token behavior.
- Today's OPD remains the shared source of status across Reception, Doctor, and Admin.
- Updated typography to a more professional Aptos/Segoe UI system font stack.
